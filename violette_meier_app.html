Three rules that apply to every book

1. Match book to platform

Angel Crush Saga belongs on BookTok. Oracles belongs in Facebook book clubs. The 366 axioms belong on Pinterest. Don't spread thin.

2. Lead with low-friction books

A new reader is more likely to spend $10 than $40. Use Preternatural Haze and the poetry books to acquire readers, then upsell into the saga.

3. Repurpose ruthlessly

One core idea per week — film once for TikTok, edit into IG reel, write long-form on Facebook. Three platforms, one hour.

About this app

A self-contained strategy tool built specifically for Violette L. Meier's catalog.

What's inside

  • All 16 books in her catalog with full audience profiles, growth tactics, and platform recommendations
  • 192 ready-to-post drafts (16 books × 3 platforms × 4 goals) with hook, caption, hashtags, and visual direction
  • Posting cadence and weekly content theme planner
  • Analytics dashboard with demo data + a toggle for her real numbers, with automatic recommendations

How to use it

  • Save it. This is a single HTML file — bookmark it, save it to her phone home screen, email it, or host it on a free service like Netlify
  • Open it in any browser. No login. No installation. Works on desktop and mobile.
  • Generate a post. Pick book, platform, goal. Copy the caption. Paste into IG/TikTok/FB. Done.
  • Plug in her real numbers when she has them — the dashboard recommendations adjust automatically.

What it doesn't do (and why)

It doesn't pull live data from Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook. Those APIs require approved Meta and TikTok developer accounts, business verification, and OAuth flows — none of which can live inside a single HTML file. If she wants real-time data, the next step is a hosted web app with backend infrastructure. This file is the strategy brain; pair it with her actual posting habits and she has a complete system.

Built around her brand

Every audience profile, post template, and hashtag was researched from her website (violettemeier.com), her book synopses, and the genres she writes in. Sadie Covington, Ma Lily, Tovah, Zayashariya, Peaches the boo hag — they're all in here by name. This isn't a generic author template. It's hers.
